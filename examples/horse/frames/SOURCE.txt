Eadweard Muybridge, The Horse in Motion (photographs 1878). Public domain.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Muybridge_race_horse_animated.gif
Frames: the 15 frames of that animation as PNG, each converted to 8-bit grey and resized to 150 x 100 with
Lanczos (Python Pillow: Image.open(f).convert('L').resize((150, 100), Image.LANCZOS)), saved as binary PGM.
They are byte-identical to the frames the GRIDPLAYER lane measured on (SETTLE/runs/gridplayer/frames/ in the
SETTLE research repository).
